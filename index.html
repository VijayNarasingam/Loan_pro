FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our business loan products

What is the minimum loan amount I can apply for?

−

The minimum loan amount varies by product type. For working capital loans, you can apply for as low as ₹5 Lakhs. For term loans and business expansion loans, the minimum is ₹10 Lakhs. Our team can help you determine the right loan amount based on your business needs.

How long does the loan approval process take?

+

Our streamlined process ensures quick approval. Once you submit your complete application with all required documents, you can expect an approval decision within 48 hours. After approval, funds are typically disbursed within 24 hours.

What is the interest rate for business loans?

+

Interest rates start from 11.5% p.a. and vary based on factors such as loan amount, tenure, business profile, credit score, and financial health.

Can I prepay my loan without penalties?

+

Yes, prepayment options are available. Charges (if any) depend on the loan product and tenure. Our team will clearly communicate applicable terms.

What documents are required for loan application?

+

Documents typically include identity proof, address proof, business registration, GST details, bank statements, and financial statements.

Is collateral required for business loans?

+

Some loan products are collateral-free, while others may require collateral depending on loan size and risk profile.

What is the maximum loan tenure available?

+

Loan tenure can range from 1 year to 7 years depending on the loan product.

Can startups apply for business loans?

+

Yes, startups with minimum operational history and financial stability can apply for eligible loan products.
